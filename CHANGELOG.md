## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-11

### Added

- First version, derived from gladys-calgary-transit (Calgary Transit).
- Next departures of every ETS stop, without any key or account: real time
  from the ETS GTFS-Realtime feed for the buses, the timetable from
  Transitous for the LRT platforms.
- Status of the Capital, Metro and Valley LRT lines, from the ETS service
  alerts.
- The closest stops suggested from the house location.
- Stop search by name or number, in the stop list shipped with the image.
- Dashboard widgets, scene triggers and scene actions (Gladys 5.1.0+).
